About Our Barangay

Barangay San Francisco is committed to transparent public service, peace and order, and programs that help families, students, and local businesses.

Residents may request clearances, join community activities, and get help from health, youth, and disaster-preparedness teams at the Barangay Hall.

Barangay Services

  • Documents

    Barangay clearance, certificate of residency, certificate of indigency, and business clearance.

  • Health Desk

    Health station visits, immunization schedules, and senior citizen assistance referrals.

  • Peace & Order

    Barangay tanod patrols, blotter assistance, and community mediation.

  • Youth & Sports

    SK programs, after-school activities, and barangay sports events.

  • Disaster Ready

    Evacuation guidance, relief coordination, and weather advisories.

  • Clean & Green

    Garbage collection schedule, recycling drive, and tree-planting days.
